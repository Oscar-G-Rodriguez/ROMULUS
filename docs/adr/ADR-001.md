# ADR-001: Weekly Trade Cadence + Daily Updates

Status: Draft

## Context
ROMULUS must define a primary decision cadence and execution timing to avoid lookahead bias and ensure operational clarity. [SOURCE_PLACEHOLDER | LOCATION-TODO | cadence guidance]

## Decision
Adopt a weekly decision cadence (Friday after close) with execution at next open (typically Monday). Daily monitoring/refresh is allowed but must not change the weekly decision package. [SOURCE_PLACEHOLDER | LOCATION-TODO | decision timing]

## Alternatives
- Fully daily decisions (rejected pending evidence)
- Monthly decisions (rejected for responsiveness)

## Consequences
- Aligns research and execution clocks.
- Requires strict cutoff enforcement in backtests and live execution.
