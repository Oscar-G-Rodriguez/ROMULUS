# ADR-006: Risk Policy + Kill-Switch Behavior

Status: Draft

## Context
ROMULUS requires defined risk limits and a kill-switch to halt trading under abnormal conditions. [SOURCE_PLACEHOLDER | LOCATION-TODO | risk controls guidance]

## Decision
Implement a global kill-switch and enforce portfolio exposure, drawdown, and loss limits. Default settings are PROVISIONAL until sources are cited. [SOURCE_PLACEHOLDER | LOCATION-TODO | risk policy]

## Alternatives
- No kill-switch (rejected)
- Manual-only controls

## Consequences
- Requires monitoring and explicit approvals for live trading.
